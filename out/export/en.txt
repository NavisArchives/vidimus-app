# Rules of use
## Article 1 — Registration
Anyone can register by showing an identity document.
The membership card is personal and may not be lend.
## Article 2 — Loans and returns
Items must be returned by the date shown on the loan receipt.
A loan can be renewed once, unless the item is reserved.
## Article 3 — Late returns
If an item is late, a reminder is sent by email. No fine is charged.
Lost or damaged items are replaced at the member's expense.
## Article 4 — Conduct
Please respect the quiet of the reading rooms.
Drinks are only allowed in the café area.
You can ask your questions at the front desk.

# Privacy policy
The library processes your personal data only to manage your membership and your loans.
## What data?
Surname, first name and date of birth
Email address, for reminders
Loan history for the last 12 month
## How long we keep it
Your data is deleted two years after your membership ends.
## Your rights
You can ask to access, correct or erase your data at any time.
For any question, write to the address shown at the front desk.
